# Blitz Rounds

Source: [https://www.hiredintech.com/algorithms/practice-ideas/blitz-rounds/](https://www.hiredintech.com/algorithms/practice-ideas/blitz-rounds/)

## Topical Practice vs Blitz Rounds

Most people do topical practice (pick a topic, solve related problems). This is useful but at interviews you don't know what topic a problem belongs to. You need to train your intuition.

## Introducing: Blitz Rounds

A focused block of practice (e.g., 2.5 hours) where you work on a pool of unknown problems. Set a fixed time per problem (e.g., 30 minutes), go through all Canvas areas, then move on.

## 4 Sample Blitz Rounds

### TopCoder Blitz Round 1
5 problems from TopCoder Arena

### TopCoder Blitz Round 2
5 more problems from TopCoder Arena

### "Cracking the Coding Interview" Blitz Round 1
Questions: 4.3, 5.2, 17.8, 17.12, 18.7

### "Cracking the Coding Interview" Blitz Round 2
Questions: 4.7, 7.6, 1.6, 17.6, 18.12

## Summary

Blitz rounds involve picking 3-5 problems, setting a time limit per problem (20-30 minutes), and sequentially filling all Canvas areas. Combined with topical practice, blitz rounds increase confidence, teach time management, and make you faster and more fluent.

# 闪电轮

来源：[https://www.hiredintech.com/algorithms/practice-ideas/blitz-rounds/](https://www.hiredintech.com/algorithms/practice-ideas/blitz-rounds/)

## 主题练习 vs 闪电轮

大多数人进行主题练习（选择一个主题，解决相关问题）。这很有用，但在面试中你不知道问题属于什么主题。你需要训练直觉。

## 介绍：闪电轮

一段集中的练习时间（例如 2.5 小时），在其中解决一组未知问题。为每道题设定固定时间（例如 30 分钟），完成画布的所有区域，然后继续下一题。

## 4 个示例闪电轮

### TopCoder 闪电轮 1
来自 TopCoder Arena 的 5 道题

### TopCoder 闪电轮 2
来自 TopCoder Arena 的另外 5 道题

### 《Cracking the Coding Interview》闪电轮 1
题目：4.3、5.2、17.8、17.12、18.7

### 《Cracking the Coding Interview》闪电轮 2
题目：4.7、7.6、1.6、17.6、18.12

## 总结

闪电轮包括选择 3-5 道题，为每道题设定时间限制（20-30 分钟），依次填写画布的所有区域。与主题练习结合，闪电轮提高自信、教你时间管理，使你更快更流畅。
